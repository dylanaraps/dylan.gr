# !

<p><i>

If you were mine my love<br>
We'd have and own a farm<br>
Raise kids and many children<br>
Dogs and cats and chickens<br>
Trees, vines and veggies<br>
I'd hold you in the garden<br>
Without a worry in the world<br>
<br>
----<br>
<br>
You're so close I could,<br>
Reach out and touch you<br>
But the void is infinite<br>
And you a million miles away<br>
<br>
There's a man in the middle<br>
He drew lines on paper<br>
And he called you Sally<br>
And me, he called a fool<br>
<br>
We're locked in our box-cages<br>
Decked out with perfumages<br>
And dumptruckloads of trinkets<br>
Sea to sea from L'Orient <br>
<br>
And if I wrote you a letter<br>
I would say you could do better<br>
Than to live your little life<br>
A fancy dancy honky tonk<br>
<br>
Seek the upwards pointing compass<br>
And through prickle and bramble<br>
Find your way to me,<br>
A million miles away<br>
<br>
I'll hold you in the garden<br>
Spiders will web beside us<br>
And they'll tell us stories<br>
Told a long long time ago<br>
<br>
Back when we were all octopods<br>
Built webs and ate flies<br>
Enjoyed the Spring breeze<br>
And the bearing Summer sun<br>
<br>
If you loved me<br>
Would you choose love<br>
Or are you party to temptation<br>
Married to the world<br>
<br>
See you later my sweet junebug<br>
I'll watch you fly away<br>
Back to your rainy slumber<br>
A million miles away<br>

</i></p>
