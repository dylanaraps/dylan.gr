# You and I

<p><i>

Old you in me<br>
And young I in you<br>
Share melancholy tales<br>
Love, loss, undo<br>
<br>
Alas go I must<br>
In search of half unmet<br>
Exist because I am<br>
Tomorrow, you forget<br>
<br>
O road of life<br>
Long, narrow, straight<br>
Make our paths cross<br>
On your road I'll wait<br>
<br>
When meet meets meet<br>
Do stay a smile<br>
Live forever you<br>
I, pain worthwhile<br>
<br>
Can you love without?<br>
Or self love self regret?<br>
A Half life lived hatred<br>
Whither die half unmet<br>
<br>
Will forces draw near?<br>
And blissful will create?<br>
And tomorrow come?<br>
Cheesy postcard fate?<br>
<br>
Paths left uncrossed<br>
Out in the world blue<br>
Have you known me?<br>
And I, known you?<br>
<br>
Will we dance?<br>
And watch ourselves dance?<br>
Will we love?<br>
Sacrifice sure life to chance?<br>
<br>
Can roots crumble rock?<br>
They can if they try<br>
Lets live forever babe<br>
Forever you and I<br>
<br>
</i></p>
