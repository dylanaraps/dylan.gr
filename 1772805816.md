# What went ye out for to see?

<p><i>

What went ye out for to see?<br>
A reed blowing in the wind?<br>
A man clothed in soft raiment?<br>
Strange whimsy, free entertainment?<br>
<br>
Stones sat upon stones?<br>
Trees amongst trees?<br>
Old house atop hill?<br>
Gentle Spring breeze?<br>
<br>
Amusement, mirth, gay merriment?<br>
Topsy, turvy, live in experiment?<br>
Divers stuff and things fantastical?<br>
Shudder at chatter ecclesiastical?<br>
<br>
What went ye out in the wilderness to see?<br>
A stick in the mud, birth of a tree?<br>
Birds and bees and desert sands?<br>
Barren homes, forgotten lands?<br>
<br>
Woman clothed by the sun.<br>
Does the sun not burn?<br>
Woman of the earth.<br>
Does the earth not swallow thee up?<br>
<br>
Fly away according to your season.<br>
Problems external, within, the reason.<br>
Flap your wings into the Summer.<br>
Flap away till pain made number.<br>
<br>
What did you come out for to see?<br>
Unknown discomfort or more known sadness?<br>
Wild adventure or tour bus madness?<br>
Feelings internal in your eyes seen.<br>
<br>
Run hide, helpless one.<br>
Lest the mirror see you.<br>
And you see the mirror.<br>
And the mirror your soul.<br>
<br>
What went ye out for to see?<br>
Me.<br>
Weak bird.<br>
Last goodbye.<br>
</i></p>
