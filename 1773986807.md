# Red Spy

<p><i>

False bells ring one spring morn.<br>
Watch stopped remains cold metal.<br>
Breaking: the red spy has fallen.<br>
<br>
Red by my windows, familiar face.<br>
Thirty daughters sleep far away.<br>
Mission impossible, alone undone.<br>
<br>
The hour come, men! Red the grass.<br>
Gather the refined white flowers<br>
Burn the rest in plumes potential.<br>
<br>
Cats scutter the station's closed.<br>
Lights out, hearth sullen, silence.<br>
I know you're there red, rusting.<br>
<br>
Gather the flock, bells a-ringing.<br>
The black robed man he's singing;<br>
The red spy has risen, long live!<br>

</i></p>
