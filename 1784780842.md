# Summer

<p>
<i>
The air is hot and the sun beats down<br>
Everything is yellow<br>
The cicadas are singing their song<br>
It feels like it'll never rain again<br>
<br>
Our only solace the shade of trees<br>
And drink of cold running water<br>
Or a swim in a fall or river<br>
And a nap to pass the midday heat<br>
<br>
---<br>
<br>
Make life sweat and boil me alive.<br>
Blind my eyes and darken my complexion.<br>
Make dry the rivers of living water<br>
And yellow the plants in my field.<br>
<!-- O source of my toil.<br> -->
<br>
Make life sweet and keep me cool.<br>
Open my eyes and lighten my countenance.<br>
Make sugar the fruit at my table<br>
And cause flowers to bloom for no reward.<br>
<!-- O source of my strength.<br> -->
<br>

<br>
Can an action occur without thought,<br>
Or is not the absence of thought in and of itself thought,<br>
And can thought occur without prior action,<br>
For what is there to think about otherwise,<br>
And if action is required for thought,<br>
And thought is reserved for the living,<br>
Then thought is a living thing,<br>
It must be physical,<br>
The dead don't walk around<br>
And the soul cannot think,<br>
And if the soul cannot think,<br>
The soul cannot know,<br>
Knowledge is of the body,<br>
The soul can only feel,<br>
And it is in this feeling that eternity lies,<br>
Thought is of the body and being<br>
Of the body, dies with it,<br>
The living know not they are alive<br>
But at best know they are not dead<br>
And the dead know nothing at all,<br>
Such is the condition of man.<br>
<br>
---<br>
<br>
O serendipitous synchronicity<br>
Shift the tracks of my destiny<br>
And send my train into the light<br>
