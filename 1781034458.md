# Ourane

<p><i>

I don't know what to do with these feelings of mine
I'm attracted to you and there's something there
But I know our differences make it nigh impossible
To see where this takes us and if love can happen

If only we had more time and you weren't French!
Or if I was French and somehow still the same me
But I know the French are only at home in France
And you can never leave it but for a short while

Why do we let bullshit get in the way of happiness?
What's more important? Public perception or life?

---

If you were in love would you choose love?
Or would you let the world get in the way?



</i></p>

-SIGNATURE
