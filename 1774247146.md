# Illusion

<p><i>

I open my eyes and begin dreaming<br>
And every dream is the same<br>
A pillar battered by chaotic forces<br>
A nail hammered down by other nails<br>
Unrelenting unreactionary disturbation<br>
<br>
I close my eyes and stop dreaming<br>
In the upper room of my mind<br>
Father, I am home. I am in the garden<br>
Find me in places not yet named<br>
And places not yet known by the dreamers<br>
<br>
I create from within and affect without<br>
For there can be no without without me<br>
Mountains are born and planets formed<br>
And seas are filled and kingdoms made<br>
Timeless, boundless and without end<br>
<br>
I become myself and you and everyone<br>
People born, dead and unborn, I am<br>
Today, I was and tomorrow I will be<br>
And of times past and yesterdays, I am<br>
In the mirror's reflection, everyone<br>
<br>
I know everything that was is and will be<br>
Every question asked is born a twin<br>
And the answer, question's younger brother<br>
Through thought I access the mind-drive<br>
Of past selfdoms and histories and phantoms<br>
<br>
The world of living and world of the dead<br>
My body the vehicle, the bridge inbetween<br>
The primal source of all things plugged in<br>
I live forever in my undreaming chamber<br>
I die and am killed in my dreams each night<br>
<br>

</i></p>
