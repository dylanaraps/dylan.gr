# Summer

<p><i>

Might thou still have the time to borrow,<br>
And room in pleasant days uncherished.<br>
Panicked face dark within dark sorrow,<br>
In barren life unlived, thou perished,<br>
And life's true blessing gone wasted;<br>
Knowing free gift heaven hath bestowed.<br>
Pain unrelenting, live life, haste it,<br>
And carry tomorrow down straight road.<br>
Broken memories come if thou remember,<br>
And only within doth change without.<br>
Pursue sweet things and fate will render,<br>
Hope, dreams and heavenly things devout.<br>
Life ends not by a bang let's not pretend,<br>
But by light autumn rain does summer end.<br>

</i></p>
